# Homework

Keeps a parent up to date on their child's school life from Smartschool: what's due on the planner, and which grades have come in.

## Planner

**Item**:
One entry on the child's Smartschool planner (a test, a piece of homework, something to bring), due on a given day.
_Avoid_: Task, assignment, planned element

**Next school day**:
The first Monday-to-Friday day after today; Friday, Saturday and Sunday all point to Monday.

**Homework report**:
The daily overview of Items: those due on the Next school day first, then the rest of the coming week.
_Avoid_: Summary, message

## Results

**Result**:
One graded evaluation of the child, as listed under "Resultaten" in Smartschool: a name, a subject, the date it was taken and a score.
_Avoid_: Grade, evaluation, mark

**Score**:
Points achieved out of points possible (e.g. `14,5/20`), together with the percentage Smartschool derives from it.

**Score colour**:
Smartschool's own verdict on a Score, one of red, yellow, green or olive (worst to best).

**Publication time**:
The moment a Result became visible to the child and parent, which can be days after the date it was taken.
_Avoid_: Result date, availability date

**Results report**:
The overview of new Results: those whose Publication time falls after the previous Results report was delivered, and no more than 14 days ago.
_Avoid_: Grades summary
